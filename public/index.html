How to use

  • Ask a question, theme, or passage problem in the inquiry box, then choose Investigate.
  • Choose a mode when you want a specific kind of work: passage finding, reference gathering, synthesis, or deep research.
  • Select works in the corpus list to narrow the investigation. Leave all unselected for the whole corpus.
  • Turn on External sources only when you want the answer enriched beyond Taylor's corpus.
  • Use citations and source cards to open Taylor passages in the reader panel.
  • Highlight Taylor wording in an answer, excerpt, or passage, then choose Show original for a Greek or Latin estimate.
  • Switch each answer between Rendered and Markdown, then use the copy icon for notes or editors.
  • Save an exchange when you want to continue the investigation later.
